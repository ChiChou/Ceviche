Fotufilm in Ceviche
===================

Ceviche's Darkroom renders film with its own film print (app/Sources/FilmPrint), which is derived from the Fotufilm
engine (https://fotufilm.com, Copyright 2026 MUAStudio Inc.) as published at
https://github.com/DhaliwalX/fotufilm-engine, commit 3a789c436716c902654f248166ebb4f580611146.

- The engine's code is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (FOTUFILM-ENGINE-LICENSE.txt, NOTICE). Ceviche
  carries a modified part of it:
  - FilmPrint/Model is the engine's FotufilmCore reduced to what Ceviche's Darkroom reaches: the stock definitions,
    the spectral model and its tables, and the plan that packs a development's parameters. The camera profiles, RAW
    decoding, lens and diffusion filters, the layered transport and film-tile grain models, the camera gate and
    border, the delivery transforms, the CPU reference renderer, pack sealing and validation were removed. A measured
    film curve keeps its highest density instead of searching its samples on every read, the stock library reads
    LZFSE-compressed definitions, and the packed configuration's layout is named in Swift (Slot.swift) rather than
    in a C header.
  - FilmPrint/Metal is Ceviche's own renderer. It replaces the engine's FotufilmHalide and FotufilmMetal modules
    (the Halide pipeline, its ahead-of-time kernels and their C++ host): the stages those define (exposure through
    the spectral cube, the emulsion's spread, halation, couplers and adjacency, grain, the print) are written as
    Metal compute kernels (FilmKernels.swift) that read the same packed configuration, with their own tiling.
    No Halide code or runtime is part of Ceviche.
  Each file taken from the engine says so at its top. The upstream THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md also describes Halide,
  browser and desktop RAW decoders and camera profiles that Ceviche does not ship.
- The film profiles in the engine's Stocks folder are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
  International (FOTUFILM_LICENSE.txt, FOTUFILM-PROFILES.txt). Ceviche ships each one with its whitespace and the
  records its film print does not read (layeredTransport, edgePrinting, crystalGrainPopulation, sheetNotches)
  removed and the result LZFSE-compressed, as `<id>.json.lzfse` (app/scripts/film-stocks.py); every number it keeps
  is as published. The repacked files are shared under the same licence. Ceviche lists the stocks by the names and
  makers they carry and leaves the three synthetic example stocks out of its list.
- THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md is the engine's own record of the third-party material it carries: of that, Ceviche ships
  the measured-reflectance prior, the projector spectrum from colour-science and the print media digitised from
  manufacturers' datasheets.
