Ceviche converts only face_landmarks_detector.tflite from the face_landmarker.task bundle to Core ML (operators mapped one to one, FP16 weights) and reshapes its outputs to 478 points and a face score.
The model set also offers an INT8 copy, its weights quantized linearly to 8 bits with one scale per output channel (models/quantize_int8.py).
